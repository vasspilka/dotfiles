We are performing a Domain audit, our goal is to ensure we a well designed Domain Model and that have developed a strong [Ubiquitous Language](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/UbiquitousLanguage.html) that helps us communicate it.

How:

1. Carefully analyze our platform then present the high level concepts of our Domain and how they are connected
2. Apply critical thinking to discover any conflicts or gaps in the model
3. Discuss the model with the user to ensure mutual understanding
4. In case of any friction work with the user to update our Domain Model and Language

$ARGUMENTS

The key for this audit is to keep in mind that we want our platform to follow Domain Driven Design principles with clear terms and language.
It's important to develop a clean Domain Model with concepts hierarchically nested to keep complexity from getting out of hand and maintaining good architecture.
